Margaretta Street

Margaretta Street was a small street in the Northern Liberties which ran parallel to Callowhill and just north of it, from Front to Second Street. Pegg's Run, over which Front Street passed by way of Pool's Bridge, lay just to the north of Margaretta Street. Between Interstate 95 and the ramps between it and Interstate 676, this small street has been completely obliterated. It was not covered by Francis White, and so the only listings are those of John MacPherson in 1785 and Clement Biddle in 1791.
