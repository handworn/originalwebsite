Green's Court

Green's Court opened north from Pine Street between 4th and 5th Streets; it seems to have been tiny, with only two occupants in 1796, the earliest date so far it is known to have existed.

(1796 Prospect) [No number then affixed to house] Francis Jackson, Mariner [African]
(1796 Prospect) [No number then affixed to house] William Buck, Porter
