Bearsticker's Alley

This wonderfully named alley, named after occupant (and probable owner) Andrew Bearsticker, opened between #68 and #70 N. 6th Street (Biddle numbering), according to the Prospect of Philadelphia (1796).

(1796 Prospect) [no number then affixed to house] Charles Brown, Labourer
(1796 Prospect) [no number then affixed to house] John M'Cauley, Taylor
(1796 Prospect) [no number then affixed to house] Elizabeth Jacobs, Widow, Seamstress
(1796 Prospect) [no number then affixed to house] Andrew Bearsticker, Watchman
