Key's Alley

Key's Alley ran from Front to 2nd, between Race and Vine. Today part of it lies under I-95; the rest is just a truncated backwater, the 100 block of New Street. There are no buildings of any description left on Key's Alley. The 1823 directory lists Key's Alley as running from #160 N. Front to #183 N. 2nd St.

Apparently Biddle's numberers thought Key's Alley, New Street and Story Street were all one street, which today they are, of course, and so numbered them consecutively.
