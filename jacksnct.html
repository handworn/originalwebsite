Jackson's Court

Jackson's Court lay on the north side of Sassafras St. (Race) between Front and 2nd. This side of this entire block contains no buildings today, only a parking lot.

(1785) #1, Jackson, Jeremiah
(1791) [No #] Jackson's Court, Jackson, Jeremiah, merchant
