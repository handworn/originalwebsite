Maiden Lane or Maiden's Row

There is no numerical overlap between the two listings, which means it's possible they may have been the same place called by two different names. This should not be taken as a proven thing, however.

(1785) #3 Maiden Lane, vacant or residents would not give name
(1785) #3 Maiden's Lane, "I won't tell you"
(1785) #6 Maiden Lane, Solliger, Jacob

(1785) #1 Maiden's Row, Course, Jacob
(1785) #2 Maiden's Row, Hoof, John
(1785) #4 Maiden's Row, McCowen, William
(1785) #5 Maiden's Row, Dawson, Edward
(1785) #10 Maiden's Row, Davis, James
