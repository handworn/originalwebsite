Budd Street

Budd Street was in the Northern Liberties; it was the part of the modern-day New Market Street that ran from Green Street up to Poplar Lane (now Poplar Street). In the 18th century, New Market Street ran only a short distance on the southern end of its later run, though the 18th century New Market Street is now almost entirely under I-95.

By 1836, Budd Street had been extended northward up to Germantown Road.
