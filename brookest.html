Brooke Street

Brooke Street was in the Northern Liberties between Green Street and Brown Street. The only listings are those from 1791:

(1791) Fichter, John, doctor of physic, Brooke St. between Green and Brown St., Northern Liberties
(1791) Grinn, George, taylor, Brooke St. between Green and Brown St., Northern Liberties
(1791) Lentz, Jacob, baker, Brooke St. between Green and Brown St., Northern Liberties
(1791) Stout, Peter, jun., butcher, Brooke St. between Green and Brown St., Northern Liberties
