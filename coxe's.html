Coxe's Alley

Coxe's Alley was a small street in Southwark, running from Front to 2nd Street, just south of Almond Street.

It was not covered by Francis White, and so the only listings would be those of John MacPherson. Clement Biddle's 1791 directory contains no listings at all under that street name; it is still unclear what he thought it was called.
