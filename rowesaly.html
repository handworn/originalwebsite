Rowe's Alley/Rose Alley

This may have been the Northern Liberties alley Biddle called Rose Alley (he included no listings for Rowe's Alley under that name), so the sole listing from Biddle for that alley is here too.

(1785) #1 Rowe's Alley, Jacobs, Moses
(1785) #2 Rowe's Alley, Douglass, Jemmet

(1791) Burkhart, Jacob, fence maker, Rose Alley
