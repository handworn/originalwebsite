Bread Street

Bread Street ran and runs north-south from Arch Street to Race Street. A few early houses (though perhaps not 18th century) remain on this street, on the west side of the northern end, near Race Street. The only listings are the 1791 listings:

EAST SIDE:

(1791) #1 Bread St., Dilhorn, George, taylor
(1791) #7 Bread St., Frank, Adam, barber
(1791) #17 Bread St., Whitesides, William, weaver
(1791) #19 Bread St., Trott, Frederick, labourer
(1791) #33 Bread St., Ives, Rachel, widow
(1791) #39 Bread St., Stow, John, turner, also at 41 Sassafras St.
