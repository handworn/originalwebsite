Story Street

Story Street ran from 3rd to 4th Street, between Race and Vine; it still exists as the 300 block of New Street. Story Street, New Street and Keys' Alley ran more or less in a line, in that order, from 4th Street on the west to Front Street on the east. White thought they were all part of New Street and mis-listed some listings of Story Street and Keys' Alley as being on New Street.

Apparently Biddle's numberers also thought Key's Alley, New Street and Story Street were all one street, which today they are, of course, and so numbered them consecutively.
