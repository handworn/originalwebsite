St. John Street

St. John Street was a street running north and south in the Northern Liberties; it is today part of American Street. It should be noted that it had nothing whatsoever to do with the Catholic saint St. John Neumann or the small alley in the Northern Liberties currently named St. John Neumann Way. The St. John the 18th century street referred to was probably the Anglican saint.
