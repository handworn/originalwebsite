Church Alley

This ran in Southwark near Old Swede's Church; it should not be confused with the Church Alley that ran (and still runs, as Church Street) next to Christ Church on 2nd Street between Market and Arch. There is one listing for some place called South Church Alley, which may have been part of Church Alley, and so is placed here until more is known.
